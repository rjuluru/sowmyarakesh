Our Story

♥

Most love stories begin with strangers. Ours? It began with family, childhood, and an endless supply of arguments. We grew up in the same family — and we knew quite literally since the day one.

Growing up, we were the definition of Tom and Jerry. Always fighting, always teasing, always getting on each other's nerves about everything — habits, friends, personality, you name it. But beneath every argument was a bond neither of us could explain. We fought like rivals and cared like no one else could.

Then one day, our families looked at us and saw what we hadn't yet — that maybe the person we'd been bickering with our whole lives was actually the one. And when we paused long enough to think about it, it all made sense. Every fight was just love that hadn't found its name yet.

"I stacked small moments like stars until destiny drew the picture exactly when it should."

— The Universe, probably

Our Journey

Since forever

Born Into the Same Story

Not everyone gets to say they've known their person since birth. We grew up in the same family — part of each other's world from day one, long before we knew what that would mean.

The Chaos Years

Tom & Jerry Era

Fights about everything. Teasing about nothing. Roasting each other's habits, friends, choices — no topic was safe. But somehow, neither of us ever stopped showing up.

The Plot Twist

Family Saw It First

Our families suggested what we hadn't dared to think. And in that moment of surprise, something quietly shifted — the fights started to feel different, the teasing a little softer.

Forever Begins

The Yes That Wrote Itself

We said yes — not to a stranger, but to the one person who already knew every flaw, every quirk, and still chose to stay. The luckiest day of our lives — so far.

Our Promise

✦

We promise to cherish the bond we've built over a lifetime of shared moments, to support each other through every chapter of our lives, and to grow together as partners, friends, and soulmates.

We promise to honor the love and trust our families have placed in us, to build a home filled with laughter, compassion, and unwavering commitment, and to face life's journey with courage and devotion.

From childhood companions to life partners — this is the beginning of forever. We stand here today, grateful for the path that led us to this moment, ready to write the next chapter of our story together.
